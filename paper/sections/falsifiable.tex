\label{sec:falsifiable}
% <- T7; D-6 is the headline figure; protocol dossier \S7.8, numbers docs/gates/T4-T7-closure.md

The architecture's central claim, that appending CST coefficients to
mfoil's Newton system yields a determined, quadratically convergent
root-find, is falsifiable by a single self-consistency experiment with a
\emph{known} answer, guaranteed realisable by construction. The protocol
fits CST to a known airfoil, runs mfoil in direct mode to generate a target
Cp/edge-velocity distribution from that fit, perturbs the coefficients (or
uses the \S\ref{sec:presolve} pre-solve) to obtain a starting guess, and
asks whether the monolithic inverse Newton iteration recovers the original
coefficient vector $A^\ast$. Any failure of this test isolates cleanly to
one of the Table~\ref{tab:fm} failure modes rather than to target
infeasibility. This is why this test, not a search over independently
chosen targets, is the load-bearing evidence for the hypothesis
(\dossierref{\S7.8}, \S7.10).

\paragraph{Winning configuration.} NACA 2412, $n=8$ per side,
\texttt{le\_treatment=prescribed} (the first $\approx$5\% chord is fixed
geometrically rather than left inverse: the ``mixed direct-inverse''
mitigation for FM-3, the cheapest test of the whole hypothesis since it
isolates the LE question from the architecture question), transition
forced (\S\ref{sec:transition}), stations chosen by QR column pivoting
(\S\ref{sec:ablations}), initialized from the T4 pre-solve, $\alpha$ fixed.

\paragraph{Protocol, including release-and-verify.} Success criterion
(dossier~\S7.8; SPEC.md~\S7, T7 row): $\|A-A^\ast\|_\infty < 10^{-4}$ within
$\leq9$ Newton iterations, with a visible quadratic tail in the D-6
convergence history. Because the inverse solve runs under the forced-
transition closure of \S\ref{sec:transition}, the protocol additionally
requires releasing transition and re-solving in fully natural (direct) mode
at the recovered geometry, verifying that the forced-transition solve did
not silently converge to an aerodynamically different answer than the
natural-transition physics would report (ADR-0003 mandate).

\paragraph{Result: PASS, with large margin (Table~\ref{tab:t7}).} Measured
against a $10^{-4}$/9-iteration gate: $\norm{A-A^\ast}_\infty =
2.747\times10^{-11}$ in 6 Newton iterations, with the target-block residual
history $5.85\times10^{-4} \to 2.05\times10^{-5} \to 1.57\times10^{-7} \to
1.21\times10^{-8} \to 2.14\times10^{-10} \to 9.47\times10^{-12}$ showing the
quadratic tail directly (Figure~\ref{fig:h3}). Release-and-verify against a
$\Delta c_l<10^{-3}$, $\Delta c_d<2\times10^{-4}$ gate gives
$\Delta c_l = 3.4\times10^{-12}$, $\Delta c_d = 3.0\times10^{-14}$, both
essentially at floating-point noise: the forced-transition solve and
the natural-transition physics agree to machine precision at the recovered
design ($c_l=0.449091$, $c_d=0.005774$ under both). Realisability
(inviscid-consistent, ADR-0004 metric 1) is $3.9\times10^{-4}$; model gap
(diagnostic only) is $0.110$ (\S\ref{sec:presolve}). Framing, per the adversarial review that closed this
gate: 16 coefficients are \emph{recovered} by the Newton iteration; the two
LE coefficients $A_{u,0}$, $A_{l,0}$ are \emph{prescribed} by the
\texttt{le\_treatment=prescribed} configuration, not recovered. This
free-16/prescribed-2 split is stated explicitly rather than folded into an
undifferentiated ``18 coefficients recovered'' claim
(docs/gates/T4-T7-closure.md).
